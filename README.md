# 👥 1Fit Consumer Insight & Market Research Analysis

An end-to-end consumer behavior study and strategic growth plan focused on bridging the gap between brand promise and actual user retention for the **1Fit** fitness subscription platform in Uzbekistan[cite: 1, 2].

---

## 📌 Project Overview

> **Course:** Consumer Behaviour Course (Group 4)[cite: 1, 2]  
> **Institution:** Inha University in Tashkent[cite: 1]  
> **Core Strategic Goal:** Shift focus from aggressive user acquisition to long-term customer retention by resolving operational service friction[cite: 1].

While 1Fit successfully hooks users with the emotional appeal of fitness variety ("try everything"), user engagement drops significantly over time[cite: 1, 2]. Based on primary research (4 in-depth interviews and 70 face-to-face surveys), our team identified that churn is driven primarily by service barriers—such as time slot restrictions and booking limits—rather than membership pricing[cite: 1, 2].

---

## 🔬 Research & Analytics Highlights

* **Sample Size:** 4 In-Depth Interviews (40–60 mins)[cite: 1, 2] + 70 face-to-face survey respondents across gyms and public areas[cite: 1].
* **The Variety Paradox:** 82.9% of users love the concept of venue variety, but 42.9% actually prefer sticking to one regular routine once excitement fades[cite: 1].
* **Service Friction vs. Price:** 68.6% consider the subscription price fair (only 24.3% complain about cost)[cite: 1]. However, churn is driven by **limited time slots (44.3%)** and **booking restrictions (38.6%)**[cite: 1].
* **Partner Gym Bias:** 31% of users report feeling treated with less respect by partner gym staff who view 1Fit members as "cheap clients"[cite: 1].
* **Churn Warning:** **26.9% of surveyed users do not plan to renew** their memberships due to service frustration[cite: 1].

---

## 🎯 Consumer Journey & Strategic Recommendations

### Consumer Journey Lifecycle
* **0–2 Months (Exploration):** High emotional excitement, active usage, and novelty seeking[cite: 1, 2].
* **3–6 Months (Transition):** Shift toward habit formation or choice overload; realization of true fitness needs[cite: 1, 2].
* **6+ Months (Retention vs. Churn):** Users either build a long-term routine or drop off due to operational friction[cite: 1, 2].

### Key Strategic Recommendations
* **Flexible Tariff Structure:** Upgrade annual plans to introduce two tiers — a standard budget plan with time limits and a premium **"1Fit+ Unlimited"** tier to eliminate peak-hour time constraints[cite: 1, 2].
* **Proactive Support & Transparency:** Launch an in-app *"What's New?"* section and push notifications for policy changes, moving away from reactive support and intrusive sales calls[cite: 1].
* **Partner Staff Relations & Fair Treatment:** Improve partner gym communications to eliminate negative staff attitudes toward 1Fit subscribers[cite: 1, 2].
